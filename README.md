# Offbeat Commit

An original 60-second instrumental jazz-reggae piece for coding, with a 9:16 vertical video.

- Video (1080x1920, 60 s): [offbeat_commit_short.mp4](https://github.com/mmajjd926-netizen/offbeat-commit/raw/main/offbeat_commit_short.mp4)
- Audio: [offbeat_commit.mp3](https://github.com/mmajjd926-netizen/offbeat-commit/raw/main/offbeat_commit.mp3) · [offbeat_commit.wav](https://github.com/mmajjd926-netizen/offbeat-commit/raw/main/offbeat_commit.wav)

76 BPM one-drop: kick and rimshot on three, swung hats and shaker, electric-piano skank on two and four,
organ bubble on the off-eighths, a warm saturated bass line and a vibraphone melody over
Dm9 – G13 – Cmaj9 – A7♭13.

Every sound is synthesized from scratch in [synth.py](synth.py) (Python, NumPy, SciPy): no samples, no loops, no AI music model.
The video is an HTML/CSS scene rendered with Playwright/Chromium, with scrolling code and an audio-reactive
spectrum made with FFmpeg (`showfreqs`).
